About Me

I’m an MSc student in Intelligent Computing Systems at Ashesi University, with a background in Computer Engineering. My work sits between academic research and applied AI, and I focus on getting models to run reliably on real hardware, from embedded devices to autonomous systems.

Before the MSc, I worked as a Data Analyst at Oze Ghana and as a Teaching Assistant at Ashesi University.
